## Brand
Her logo is a black square with a white brain mark (two halves, circuit lines) and her name. The nav uses a tile made from it (brain mark only; the small text disappears at icon size) plus «NIKOLETA TSOMPANIDOU / ΨΥΧΟΛΟΓΟΣ MSc» in spaced capitals, as on her logo. The tile is also the favicon, app icon and social card logo.
